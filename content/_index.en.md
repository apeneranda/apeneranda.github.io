Adrien Peneranda is Associate Professor (Maître de conférences) of Management at [Sciences Po Toulouse](https://www.sciencespo-toulouse.fr/) and a member of [LEREPS](https://lereps.sciencespo-toulouse.fr/) (EA 4212). His research covers public management, information systems management and the philosophy of management. His doctoral work (Aix-Marseille University, 2014) dealt with knowledge commons and the public governance of cultural data, in the wake of Elinor Ostrom and Herbert Simon; he now studies the ethical foundations of managerial action (Aristotle, Hannah Arendt, Marcel De Corte). From 2021 to 2026 he served as Deputy Director of Sciences Po Toulouse in charge of degree programmes. He is the editor of the Marcel De Corte collection (Hora Decima).
