Adrien Peneranda has been Associate Professor of Management (maître de conférences, CNU section 06) at [Sciences Po Toulouse](https://www.sciencespo-toulouse.fr/) since 2015 and is a member of the [Laboratory for the Study of Economics, Policies and Social Systems](https://lereps.sciencespo-toulouse.fr/) (LEREPS, EA 4212).

A graduate of [IESEG School of Management](https://www.ieseg.fr/) (major in information systems management) and an auditor of the 65th national session of the [Institute for Advanced National Defence Studies](https://ihedn.fr/) (IHEDN), he obtained the Research Master's in Management Sciences of [IMPGT](https://impgt.univ-amu.fr/) ([Aix-Marseille University](https://www.univ-amu.fr/)) in 2010, ranking first in his class. His doctoral thesis, *Biens communs et management public du patrimoine culturel à l'ère numérique* [Commons and Public Management of Cultural Heritage in the Digital Age], supervised by Serge Amabile and defended at Aix-Marseille University in 2014, received the IMPGT Thesis Prize. He then worked as a Temporary Lecturer and Researcher (ATER) at IMPGT and at [ESM-IAE Metz](https://iaemetz.univ-lorraine.fr/) ([University of Lorraine](https://www.univ-lorraine.fr/)).

His research covers three related fields:
- **public management and information systems**: public data governance, decision support systems for local government, digital transformation of public administrations;
- **the commons**: governance of knowledge commons and collective action, in the wake of Elinor Ostrom;
- **philosophy of management**: moral action and management in Aristotle, Hannah Arendt's *vita activa*, the work of Marcel De Corte.

He has published in *Systèmes d'information et management*, *La Revue des sciences de gestion*, *Développement durable et territoires* and the *Revue thomiste*, among others. He co-edited *Fonction(s) publique(s) : le défi du changement* [Public Service(s): The Challenge of Change] ([Presses de l'EHESP](https://www.presses.ehesp.fr/), 2018) and is the series editor of the Marcel De Corte collection at Hora Decima; he also sits on the scientific board of the Critères book series with the same publisher.

At Sciences Po Toulouse, he served as Director of Continuing Education Development and Digital Strategy (2016–2019), Officer for internships and employability (2020–2021), and Deputy Director for Academic Programmes (2021–2026). He is a member of the Governing Board of Sciences Po Toulouse. He teaches business strategy, international human resource management, information systems and the digital transformation of public organisations.
